\paragraph{The Well Tempered Typewriter, by Sadie Philippa Kaye.} %1
C~major. Simple, introductory, musical.

\paragraph{Death of a Piano, by Brynneth Nimue Brown.} %2
Memory, melancholy and loss.

\paragraph{(No) Lingua Franca, by Irena Peterson Pavliuk.} %3
F~minor: Melancholy. Sombre, emotional.

\paragraph{Piper Finchling, by Steven C. Davis.} %4
Exploration, wonder and curiosity.

Ominous and Wagnerian, in E\fl~major, as for the beginning of \textit{Das Rheingold}.

\paragraph{The Room, by Irena Peterson Pavliuk.} % 6
D~minor: Confinement, restlessness, absorption, irreconcilability, legacy, metamorphosis.

\paragraph{Being Someone, by Sadie Philippa Kaye} % 7
Mock heroic, in E\fl~minor, but with distress, fighting the despair.
