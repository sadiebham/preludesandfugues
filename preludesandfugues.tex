\section*{Sadie Kaye}

Sadie Kaye, \texttt{https://sadie.org.uk} and \texttt{sadie314159@gmail.com}, is an author, poet, artist, 
musician and feminist. Her list of favourite poets includes Wallace Stevens, Stevie Smith and others.  She is 
particularly interested in mythology and folk tales and has recently published `From Earth and Sky,' a poetic 
reconstruction and commentry on Norse mythology, `The Mermaid of Purt-le-Moirrey,' a folk tale from the Isle of 
Man, and `The Goddess Sings,' a set of poetic monologues and dialogues from Mythology, including Sumerian, 
Greek, Danish, Welsh and Irish.  These books are available on Amazon. She is currently working on a novel and a 
further collection of poetry. mirth happiness teasing

\section*{Steven C.~Davis}

Steven C. Davis describes themselves as a word witch. They have created the `Sælvatici Mythos' [Robin Hood myths 
in a folk-horror/dark Pagan style] and the `Less than Human’ [an examination of society through the medium of 
genetically-based shape shifters] worlds. They are the Creative Director of Tenebrous Texts and are also a 
storyteller, a performance poet, a publisher, a playwright, a presenter, an artist, a master of ceremonies, and 
are occasionally considered driven. They are near-infinitely patient, ace, darkly-inclined and feel malevolent 
joy frequently. For ‘Piper Finchling’ it was a delight to write something in an entirely different style.
